**Target repo:** `/data/ipl/ipl27/sfernandez/headsize_sexeffects_ukb/`
**Reason for handoff:** The sex imbalance originates in the upstream UKB
normative pipeline (QC + ICD-10 filtering on the UKB imaging cohort).
That manuscript already documents the 56% female sample and the
PRISMA-style flow diagram. A by-sex breakdown of the flow belongs there,
not in this AD manuscript, which only consumes the normative reference.

This document specifies what to add upstream so the audit is reproducible
and reportable in the source paper.

**File:** `reports-src/manuscript.qmd`

### 2a. PRISMA / flow diagram

The flow diagram is referenced at line ~166 (`@fig-prisma`). Two options:

**Option A (lightweight):** Add a single Female/Male row under each node
in the existing PRISMA flow.

**Option B (preferred, more readable):** Replace the single-track PRISMA
with a two-column flow (one per sex), each showing the per-stage Ns
generated by the audit script. This makes the differential attrition
immediately legible.

### 2b. Methods text

Near the existing ICD-10 paragraph (around line 162), add ~2 sentences:

> "Quality-control attrition was sex-asymmetric: DARQ scan-level failure
> rates were 24.0% in males versus 13.0% in females, while AssemblyNet
> grade-failure rates were comparable across sexes (0.5% vs 0.7%). Net
> attrition from the source imaging cohort (52.0% female) to the final
> analytic sample (56% female) is therefore driven primarily by
> motion-sensitive automated QC rather than diagnostic exclusions."

(Adjust "motion-sensitive" if the DARQ paper does not support that
attribution — see Caveats below.)

## 4. Caveats to address before claiming mechanism

Two attributions in §2b above need verification before reporting them as
mechanistic claims:

1. **DARQ as motion-sensitive.** Check `darq_*.csv` provenance and cite
   the DARQ paper (likely Mouches et al. or the Dadar group's QC
   pipeline) to confirm what DARQ scores. If DARQ is a general
   appearance-based QC rather than motion-specific, soften the text to
   "automated appearance-based QC."
2. **Sex-pattern of psychiatric flags in UKB.** Slightly more females
   have F-code flags in the UKB imaging cohort (1,007 vs 899). Before
   attributing this to known UKB depression/anxiety patterns, check the
   linked-records exclusion source (HES vs self-report) and decide
   whether mechanism-level commentary is worth including or whether the
   directional fact alone is sufficient.
